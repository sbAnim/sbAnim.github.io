Role Breakdown

At We Are Royale, I was brought on to help contextualize 3D assets provided by Riot Games for “Summit,” a piece showcasing a Valorant map. Working alongside the team, I contributed to previs, 3D rendering, and projecting illustrations onto the environment, helping shape the visual language and bring the final piece together.
